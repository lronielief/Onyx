Responsible Gaming Policy

Hospitality first. Player protection always. At ONYX, we believe gaming should remain an enjoyable form of leisure. We are committed to preventing gambling-related harm through rigorous safeguards, education, and proactive player support.

Self-Exclusion Program Helpline & Support

Our four foundational safeguards

01

Strict 21+ Age Limit & KYC Verification

Access to all gaming salons is restricted to guests aged 21 and above. Government-issued photo identification is mandatory at every entry checkpoint. Minors are strictly prevented from entering gaming floors and observing active play.

02

National Exclusion Database (NDEP)

We actively participate in PAGCOR's National Database of Excluded Persons (NDEP). Individuals registered under voluntary self-exclusion or family exclusion are systematically checked and denied access to all property gaming areas.

03

Financial Safeguards & Anti-Coercion

ONYX does not extend credit or encourage aggressive wagering. No ATMs are situated directly within salon gaming pits, ensuring guests step away from gaming areas before accessing additional funds.

04

Staff Intervention & Care Protocols

Our floor attendants, pit bosses, and security teams are formally trained in identifying signs of gambling distress, offering discreet breaks, and facilitating immediate contact with counselling resources.

How to enroll in the Self-Exclusion Program

Step 1

Self-Exclusion Application

An individual may voluntarily apply for exclusion for 3 months, 6 months, or up to 5 years. The application form can be submitted directly through our Responsible Gaming Officer or via PAGCOR.

PAGCOR RG Forms →
Step 2

Family Exclusion Request

Immediate family members (spouse, parents, or adult children) may apply to exclude a relative experiencing gambling addiction. Documentation of relationship and legitimate grounds is required.

Contact PAGCOR RG →
Step 3

Nationwide Enforcement

Once processed, the exclusion is enforced across all licensed gaming facilities in the Philippines, barring entry and cancelling any loyalty rewards or marketing communications.
